**Summary statistics for longitudinal genotype by time cis-eQTL interaction analyses**. Longitudinal linear mixed effects models included an interaction term for genotype dosage at each assessed variant and time from baseline visit. Variant-gene pairs with genotype x time cis-eQTL interaction significant at a 5% Benjamini-Hochberg FDR are shown. P-values correspond to the genotype x time interaction term.
