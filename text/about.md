# Welcome to the MultiMuTHER study

MultiMuTHER is a longitudinal multi-omic study designed to investigate how molecular traits - such as gene expression and serum metabolite levels - change over time within individuals. The study includes 335 female participants from the TwinsUK registry, each of whom has provided samples at three or more clinical visits.

The cohort includes both monozygotic and dizygotic twins. Multi-omic profiling currently consists of whole-blood bulk RNA sequencing and metabolomic profiling on the Metabolon platform, complemented by extensive genotypic, clinical, biochemical, and anthropometric data. Together, these datasets provide a rich resource for understanding molecular changes over time.

The MultiMuTHER project is led by [Julia El-Sayed Moustafa](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/people/julia-el-sayed-moustafa) and [Kerrin Small](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/people/kerrin-small) at King’s College London.
